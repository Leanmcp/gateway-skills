# Instagram

Visual-first, and the least natural home for technical launches. Worth doing when the material is genuinely visual (a UI, a demo, a chart, a physical thing, a team) or when the audience is founders, designers, students, or a consumer product's users. Do not force a backend infrastructure release onto Instagram. It will underperform and it will look like it was posted out of obligation.

## Format rules

**Caption length.** 2,200 characters hard cap. Roughly the first 125 characters show before "...more". That first line does the same job as LinkedIn's truncation point.

**The image carries it.** Unlike LinkedIn and X, the caption is secondary. If the visual is weak, no caption saves it. Budget the effort accordingly.

**Do not repeat the slide text in the caption.** If slide 1 says "We cut eval time by 94%", the caption should not open with "We cut eval time by 94%". Use the caption for the story behind it.

**Hashtags.** The cap is 30, which is also a trap. Three to five specific ones outperform a block of thirty generic ones, and a thirty-tag block reads as spam. Put them at the end of the caption or in the first comment.

**Line breaks.** Instagram collapses multiple blank lines inconsistently in the composer. If the caption needs spacing, write it in a plain text editor and paste it in.

**Alt text** is under advanced settings when posting. Add it.

## Asset specs

| Asset | Dimensions | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed post (default) | 1080 x 1350 | 4:5 | Recommended. Most vertical space without cropping. |
| Feed post (square) | 1080 x 1080 | 1:1 | Safe, older look |
| Feed post (landscape) | 1080 x 566 | 1.91:1 | Least feed space, avoid |
| Profile grid preview | 1080 x 1440 | 3:4 | What the grid crops to; check your 4:5 posts survive it |
| Carousel | 1080 x 1350 | 4:5 | Up to 20 slides. Use one ratio for every slide or they crop unevenly. |
| Reels | 1080 x 1920 | 9:16 | |
| Stories | 1080 x 1920 | 9:16 | |

**Safe zones.** On Reels and Stories, keep text and faces out of the top ~250 px and bottom ~420 px. That is where the username, caption, action buttons and progress bars sit. Content there gets covered.

**Reels.** Vertical, 9:16. Keep it under 30 seconds for a launch clip unless the demo genuinely needs longer. The first second decides retention, so open on the output, not on a title card. Add burned-in captions; most viewers watch muted.

**Carousel cover.** Slide 1 is the only slide most people see. It has to make the swipe worth it on its own.

## Post shapes that work

**The carousel explainer.** 6 to 10 slides, one idea per slide, large type, high contrast. Works for papers, concepts, and before/after.

**The demo Reel.** Screen recording of the thing working, 15 to 30 seconds, captions burned in, no talking-head intro.

**The behind-the-scenes.** Whiteboard, messy desk, the bug that took three days. Performs well and is the format where Instagram's informality is an advantage rather than a mismatch.

**Story sequence.** 3 to 5 frames on launch day, with a link sticker on the last one. Lowest effort, decent reach with an existing following.

## Worked example caption

```
the eval runner used to lose everything when a task crashed

three hours of re-running, every time, just to find out what the model had
actually done on turn 4

swipe for what we changed

(the honest version: most of what we found was not the model being wrong. it
was our own harness timing out at 30 seconds on steps that took 90)

repo in bio

#opensource #mlops #devtools
```

Caption is 380 characters, three hashtags, no emoji, no em dashes, and the parenthetical gives the reader something the slides do not.
